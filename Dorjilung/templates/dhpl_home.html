Project Introduction

The Dorjilung Hydroelectric Power Project (DHPP) is one of Bhutan's most important national infrastructure initiatives. It is envisioned as a transformational 1,125 MW run-of-river project that will significantly strengthen the country's long-term energy security, economic development, and commitment to low-carbon growth. Recognized as a national priority by His Majesty the King, DHPP represents a modern approach to hydropower development, one that emphasizes strong environmental and social safeguards, adherence to international best practices, and a transparent Public-Private Partnership model between Druk Green Power Corporation Limited (DGPC) and Tata Power Company Limited of India. Supported by the financial and technical expertise of the World Bank Group, the project is expected to become a leading example of sustainable hydropower development in South Asia.

Project Overview

Transforming the Lives of the People of Bhutan

The Dorjilung Hydroelectric Power Project (DHPP) is a 1,125 MW run-of-the-river project located on the Kurichhu River in Lhuentse Dzongkhag. As a joint initiative with DGPC, Tata Power, and support from the World Bank, DHPP represents a significant step towards energy security and economic prosperity for Bhutan.

Partnership Model and Financing

The project is being developed through a joint venture between DGPC and TCPL, where DGPC will hold 60 percent equity and TCPL will hold 40 percent. A dedicated company, Dorjilung Hydro Power Limited (DHPL), has been incorporated to develop, operate, and maintain the project under a concession agreement with the Royal Government of Bhutan for a period of 30 years.

Financing and International Standards

DHPP has an estimated cost of USD 1.7 billion. The financing structure will follow a debt-equity ratio of seventy percent debt and thirty percent equity. The World Bank Group will serve as the lead multilateral financier.

Sustainability

Environment & Sustainability

At Dorjilung Hydropower Company Limited (DHPL), we are deeply committed to environmental stewardship. The Dorjilung Hydroelectric Power Project (DHPP) is designed and operated with a profound respect for Bhutan's pristine natural environment, which is central to our national identity and the well-being of our people. We believe that sustainable development is the only viable path forward, and we strive to ensure that our project contributes positively to the environment and the communities we serve.

Our Environmental Commitment

Our commitment to the environment is embedded in every phase of the project, from planning and construction to operation and decommissioning. We adhere to the highest international standards and best practices to minimize our environmental footprint. Our comprehensive Environmental and Social Management System (ESMS) ensures that all potential impacts are identified, assessed, and mitigated effectively.

Key Environmental Initiatives

  • Biodiversity Conservation: We are implementing a robust Biodiversity Management Plan to protect and enhance the rich flora and fauna in the project area. This includes habitat restoration, species monitoring, and the establishment of conservation corridors.
  • Water Quality Management: We employ state-of-the-art systems to maintain the quality of the Kurichhu River. Continuous monitoring and treatment ensure that the river's ecological balance is preserved.
  • Air Quality Control: During construction, we utilize dust suppression techniques and emission control measures to safeguard local air quality and the health of nearby communities.
  • Waste Management: We follow a comprehensive waste management plan that prioritizes reduction, reuse, and recycling. All waste is handled in an environmentally sound manner.
  • Climate Change Mitigation: As a clean energy project, DHPP significantly reduces greenhouse gas emissions compared to fossil fuel-based power generation. We are proud to contribute to Bhutan's commitment to remain carbon-neutral and support global climate goals.

International Standards & Best Practices

DHPP is guided by Good International Industry Practice (GIIP) and aligns with the Environmental, Health, and Safety (EHS) Guidelines of the World Bank Group. We have also adopted the Hydropower Sustainability Assessment Protocol (HSAP) to ensure that our project meets the highest environmental and social standards. Our commitment to excellence is reflected in our adherence to ISO 14001:2015, the international standard for environmental management systems.

Community & People

Social Responsibility

At Dorjilung Hydropower Company Limited (DHPL), we recognize that our success is intertwined with the well-being of the communities we serve. The Dorjilung Hydroelectric Power Project (DHPP) is not just an engineering marvel; it is a catalyst for social progress and a testament to our commitment to inclusive and equitable development. We are dedicated to ensuring that the project brings lasting benefits to the people of Lhuentse Dzongkhag and contributes to the broader socio-economic fabric of Bhutan.

Our Social Commitment

Our social commitment is built on the principles of transparency, fairness, and respect for human rights. We engage with stakeholders in a meaningful and culturally appropriate manner, ensuring that their voices are heard and their concerns are addressed. Our approach is guided by international best practices, including the World Bank's Environmental and Social Standards (ESS).

Key Social Initiatives

  • Community Engagement & Stakeholder Participation: We maintain an open and continuous dialogue with affected communities, local leaders, and civil society. Regular meetings, public consultations, and information dissemination sessions are held to ensure transparency and build trust.
  • Land Acquisition & Livelihood Restoration: We have implemented a comprehensive Land Acquisition and Livelihood Restoration Plan (LALRP) that provides fair compensation at replacement cost. We support affected households with livelihood restoration programs, transitional allowances, and access to new opportunities.
  • Local Employment & Skills Development: We prioritize the employment of local people in project-related activities. Our vocational training programs equip community members with valuable skills, enhancing their employability and contributing to local economic growth.
  • Cultural Heritage Preservation: We respect and protect the cultural heritage and traditions of the local communities. Our Cultural Heritage Management Plan ensures that sacred sites and cultural practices are preserved and respected.
  • Grievance Redressal Mechanism (GRM): We have established a robust GRM to address any concerns or complaints from the community. The mechanism is accessible, transparent, and responsive, with multiple channels for submitting grievances.
  • Community Health & Safety: We implement community health and safety plans to mitigate risks associated with the project. This includes awareness campaigns, health camps, and close coordination with local health authorities.

Gender Equality & Social Inclusion

DHPL is committed to promoting gender equality and social inclusion. We have developed a Gender and Vulnerability Action Plan to address the specific needs and concerns of women, youth, the elderly, and other vulnerable groups. We ensure that women have equal access to employment opportunities, training, and participation in decision-making processes. Our project aims to empower women and contribute to a more just and equitable society.
